# Candidate Words — One Word From Grandma

The bounty asks for the single word Grandma sent about AI. Every candidate
below was scored out of 25 against what the judge is looking for: the word
has to be clearly *about AI*, sound like a real grandma, and be funny on
its own, with no setup or explanation attached.

## Why ChatGTP wins

Three things separate it from the rest of the field:

1. **It is the most tied to AI.** The other candidates describe Grandma's *reaction* to AI — magic, ghosts, appliances, scolding. ChatGTP *is* the AI's name, typed wrong. The subject is baked into the word itself.
2. **The typo is the grandma voice.** She is not performing confusion; the swapped letters are the confusion. Warm, genuine, and a little lost — exactly how a grandma actually texts.
3. **It is funny instantly, with zero context.** A reader who sees only the word still gets the joke: "ChatGPT, misspelled." No other candidate lands without the reader first knowing what the question was.

The field already contains robut, robits, Contraption, DOOHICKEY, and Robocall — all variations on "strange machine". ChatGTP takes a different, fresher angle: not the machine, but the name Grandma was trying to say.
